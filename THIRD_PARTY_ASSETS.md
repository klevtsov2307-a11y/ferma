# Imported visual assets

Source: https://github.com/ana3stay/3d-farm
Source revision: 0f38c78b84c5273822d78a42f6909b09a64459a4
Imported at the project owner's explicit request, 2026-11-07.

Files under dist/art/imported are derived from Assets/Resources and
Assets/simple_low_poly_village_buildings in that repository.
FBX geometry was projected to SVG with sampled material colors and directional
lighting; JPEG textures were converted to WebP embedded in SVG containers.
The interactive Farm-off application code remains separate from Unity scripts.

No LICENSE or asset-specific permission notice was found in the source repository.
This attribution is not a license grant. Commercial redistribution permissions
and third-party asset provenance remain unverified. The site remains private.
Music and executables were not imported.

Additional adaptation: house variants 2, 5 and 7; a single red vegetable with its leaves extracted from disconnected mesh components 71/72 of farm_lvl3.FBX, rendered as tomato-single.svg. These assets do not establish any new license permissions.
